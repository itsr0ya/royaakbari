NaviCare Mobile App

NaviCare is a mobile app designed to help vulnerable individuals easily find and navigate essential support services such as housing, legal, etc.

Project Type:

Group (3 people)

Project Duration:

6 Weeks

My role:

UX Designer

Problem

Finding essential support can be overwhelming. People may not know which services exist or what they’re called, while scattered information, language barriers and complex processes make access harder.

Outcome

NaviCare helps users discover relevant services based on their circumstances and location, without needing to know what service to search for in the first place.

Target Audience

The primary audience are newly arrived refugees and migrants, people experiencing homelessness, and individuals facing financial stress.

Tools and Technologies

Figma was used throughout the design process, from early sketching and low-fidelity wireframes to developing high-fidelity prototypes. It supported the exploration of layout ideas, user flows, and iterative refinements, allowing designs to evolve based on feedback and testing.
